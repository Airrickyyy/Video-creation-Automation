# 01 — Ideas

```
You're helping me plan faceless YouTube Shorts (vertical, 15–35 seconds, AI narration,
captions, still images or stock clips). Niche: {{NICHE, e.g. "space and science history"}}.

Give me 10 video ideas. Each must be ONE small, true story with a surprising turn,
not a list of facts. Avoid topics that are already done to death in this niche
({{list any you've already made or seen too often}}).

For each idea give:
- Working title (under 50 characters)
- The hook: the first sentence the narrator says. It must state the most surprising thing
  immediately, with no intro, no "did you know", no "in this video".
- The payoff: the final line, which ideally loops back into the hook so the video replays naturally
- Why it holds attention (the open question the viewer wants answered)
- Visuals: can this be shown with public-domain or freely licensed images/footage?
  Name likely sources (NASA, Library of Congress, Wikimedia Commons public domain, Pexels).
- Fact risk: which claims need checking, and how well documented the story is

Then rank all 10 by: hook strength, how easy it is to show visually with legal assets,
how verifiable the facts are, and originality. Score each 1–5 on those four and
show the total. Recommend the top pick.
```
