# Playbook

What we've learned works. The prompts in `prompts/` should follow these rules.
Every rule cites where it came from; update it as our own analytics come in
(our own numbers beat anyone else's).

Last updated: 2026-10-21 — based on general research + 8 reference Shorts. No own data yet.

## Story
- One story or one answer per video. Never a list. *(library/2026-10-21: lists 3–7K vs single stories 78K–4.96M)*
- First sentence = a conflict or action already happening, with a person and stakes. No question openers like "What's the dumbest...". *(library/2026-10-21, docs/RESEARCH.md)*
- Relatable stakes (family, school, work, fairness) over shock. *(library/2026-10-21)*
- End with a payoff: twist, verdict, or mid-sentence cliffhanger that loops into the start. *(library/2026-10-21)*
- 20–45 seconds unless the story has a real mystery that earns more. *(library/2026-10-21, docs/RESEARCH.md)*

## Title
- A line or quote from the story, or a strong judgment. Not hashtags. *(library/2026-10-21)*

## Visuals
- Constant motion. For story formats, bright gameplay we recorded ourselves. *(library/2026-10-21)*
- Big centered captions, 1–3 words at a time, key words colored. *(library/2026-10-21)*

## Rules we don't break
- Original writing only; never read someone else's post word for word.
- Only footage we recorded or have a license for, logged in `sources.json`.
- Every video gets owner approval before upload.
