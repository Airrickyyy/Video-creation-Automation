# Playbook

What we've learned works. The prompts in `prompts/` should follow these rules.
Every rule cites where it came from; update it as our own analytics come in
(our own numbers beat anyone else's).

Last updated: 2026-10-22 — general research + 8 reference Shorts + niche study of ~250 videos
(see `library/`). No own data yet.

## Pick a format on purpose
Two formats clearly work in this niche; middle-length rambling stories do worst. *(library/2026-10-22)*
- **Drama:** a big premise, 2.5–3 min, fast narration, satisfying ending.
- **Joke:** one everyday situation + punchline, under 30s.

## Story
- One story or one answer per video. Never a list. *(library/2026-10-21: lists 3–7K vs single stories 78K–4.96M)*
- First sentence = the whole premise, with a person, stakes and a specific detail. No question openers. *(library/2026-10-21, 2026-10-22)*
- Second hook in the first 5 seconds: flash forward to the trouble ("The sheriff showed up on hour 26"). *(library/2026-10-22)*
- Use specific numbers ($600,000, "ranked 74 of 87"). *(library/2026-10-22)*
- Relatable stakes (family, school, work, money, fairness). *(library/2026-10-21, 2026-10-22)*
- **Always end with a payoff**: twist, verdict, justice, or punchline. Stories that stop mid-drama flop. *(library/2026-10-22: 24K vs 1.2M on the same channel)*
- No shock bait: it gets clicks but ~0.2% like rates and policy risk. *(library/2026-10-22)*
- Original only. Famous reposted posts did worse and risk the reused-content rules. *(library/2026-10-22)*

## Title
- Drama: the first sentence, word for word. Joke: a short reaction + one emoji. Never hashtags-only. *(library/2026-10-21, 2026-10-22)*
- 2–4 hashtags at the end of the description, not stuffed in the title. *(library/2026-10-22)*

## Delivery
- Fast narration: ~270–310 words per minute for drama. *(library/2026-10-22)*
- The hook is on screen at 0.0s. *(library/2026-10-22)*

## Visuals
- Constant motion, bright. Minecraft parkour or Trackmania, recorded by us. *(library/2026-10-21, 2026-10-22)*
- **Our house style: the growing white post card** (community header, votes/comments bar) with
  small captions at the bottom. Owner's choice after seeing the alternatives (2026-10-22).
- Other styles exist in the code (title card + one-word captions, chat box) but the owner didn't
  like them; only revisit if our own analytics say the house style underperforms. *(library/2026-10-22)*

## Rules we don't break
- Original writing only; never read someone else's post word for word.
- Only footage we recorded or have a license for, logged in `sources.json`.
- Every video gets owner approval before upload.
