An octopus has three hearts. Two of them pump blood through the gills, and the third pumps it to the rest of the body. Its blood is blue, because it carries oxygen with copper instead of iron. And most of its neurons are not in its head at all. They are spread through its eight arms, which can taste and feel on their own. So the next time you see an octopus, remember: you are looking at one of the strangest minds on Earth.
