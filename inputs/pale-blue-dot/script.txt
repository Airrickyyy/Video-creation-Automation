This speck of light is every person who has ever lived.

On Valentine's Day, 1990, Voyager 1 was more than four billion miles from home.

Carl Sagan had pushed NASA to turn its camera around one last time.

Earth came out smaller than a single pixel.
By pure chance, it landed inside a beam of scattered sunlight.

Soon after, the cameras were shut off to save power.
These were the last pictures Voyager 1 ever took.

Every war, every love story, every name you have ever heard happened right there.
Look again.
