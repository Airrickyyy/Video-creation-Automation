My little brother sold my gaming PC for *forty dollars* while I was at work.
He's *nine*. He listed it himself, met the buyer on our porch, and handed it over.
I came home to an *empty desk* and a jar with two twenties in it.
I was *furious*, until I saw the note taped to the jar.
It said, "For your *birthday*. Now you can buy a real one."
He thought forty dollars was a *fortune*.
I still have both twenties.
I don't think I'll *ever* spend them.
