My landlord kept my entire $2,400 deposit because of one nail hole. Three weeks later, he was the one begging me to settle.
I rented his apartment for four years and paid on time, all forty-eight months.
When I moved out, I cleaned for two days straight and patched the one nail hole from my calendar.
A week later his letter came. Repainting, eight hundred dollars. New carpet, twelve hundred. Cleaning fee, four hundred.
That carpet was the same one I moved in with. It was already eleven years old.
What he didn't know was that on move-in day, I took *one hundred and twelve* photos. Every wall, every stain, every scratch, all with timestamps.
I sent him a polite email with every photo attached. He replied with one word. *Lawyer.*
So I filed in small claims court. The filing fee was seventy-five dollars.
The judge flipped to photo forty-one. The same carpet stain, four years apart. She asked him one question. Why did you charge my client for this?
He didn't have an answer.
I walked out with my full deposit, plus the filing fee, plus a *penalty* for keeping it in bad faith.
Last I heard, he takes move-in photos now too.
