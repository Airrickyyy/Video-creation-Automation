My grandma charged me *five dollars* a week for wifi for three years.
I was sixteen and broke, so I complained every single *Sunday*.
She'd just hold out her hand and say, "House rules."
Last month I turned *nineteen* and moved into my first apartment.
On moving day, she handed me a *shoebox*.
Inside was every five dollar bill I had ever paid her.
Seven hundred and *eighty dollars*, with a date written on each one.
Taped to the lid was a note. "First month's internet. *House rules*."
